Coregistration pipeline

Source + reference images

Key points from reference and source are connected

A transformation function, the homography, is computed from the matched points.

  • A homography is a 3×3 matrix that maps source positions onto the reference.
  • The image is split into small cells, and each cell gets its own homography. Click a cell to see its matrix:

A few local homography matrices come out noisy.

  • Bad matches corrupt a few cells, and their noisy matrices cause ghosting in the warp.
  • We apply median filtration on top of the local homography matrices.
  • Click a red cell to see its noisy matrix, then switch to the filtered field:

Pixels are projected through the homography transformation function.

  • Each pixel of the aligned image is traced back through its cell's homography to a position in the source image.
  • It takes the colour found there, so the source lands exactly on the reference.

Overlapping pieces are merged into one seamless image.
